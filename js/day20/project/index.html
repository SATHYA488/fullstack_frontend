College Presentation Summary Dossier

Future Without IT vs Future With IT

Executive project abstract, key comparative findings, and strategic takeaways.

1. Project Objective & Core Concept

This project conducts a comprehensive comparative inquiry into the pivotal role of Information Technology in shaping human civilization. By contrasting a future empowered by IT against a counterfactual world without IT, it illustrates why technology is essential for solving planetary challenges while outlining guardrails against catastrophic technological risks.

2. Key Domain Takeaways

  • Healthcare: AI predictive diagnostics and robotic surgery boost life expectancy to 90+, whereas a world without IT suffers severe delays and preventable epidemic mortality.
  • Education: Immersive VR spatial learning and adaptive AI mentors democratize universal literacy, compared to extreme knowledge isolation with physical paper textbooks.
  • Commerce & Finance: Instant quantum finance prevents liquidity paralysis, whereas a cash-only economy shrinks global trade by over 70%.
  • Mobility: Synchronized V2X autonomous networks eradicate 1.3M annual traffic fatalities.

3. Ethical Governance Framework

To overcome job displacement, cybersecurity vulnerabilities, and privacy erosion, societies must enforce Privacy-by-Design, lifelong reskilling programs, green renewable computing architectures, and human-in-the-loop governance.
