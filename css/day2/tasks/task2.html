zoho company


Zoho Corporation is a heavily bootstrapped, privately held Indian multinational that develops cloud-based business software. Founded in 1996 and headquartered in Chennai, India, the company boasts over 150 million users worldwide. They offer an extensive suite of over 55 applications, including CRM, Mail, Desk, and Books.

core pillers

  • Bootstrapped & Profitable: Unlike many heavily funded tech counterparts, Zoho has grown entirely on its own revenue without external venture capital.
  • Privacy-First: The company does not generate revenue via advertising or user data tracking; its sole income comes from software license fees.
  • Research & Development: Zoho maintains a highly integrated technology stack, even building and hosting its own global data centers and proprietary hardware (like in-house servers)

Major Product Suites

  • Zoho Workspace: A comprehensive suite of office and productivity applications, including Zoho Mail, WorkDrive, and Writer.
  • Zoho CRM: A widely utilized customer relationship management platform designed for sales and marketing.
  • ManageEngine: An enterprise IT management division offering IT operations, security (SIEM), and endpoint management.
  • Qntrl: A dedicated workflow orchestration platform to automate business processes.

Recent Updates & Innovations

Zoho continues to expand its technological independence and global footprint. Recently, the company deployed its first data centers in the UAE, launched a POS retail division called Zakya, and invested heavily in sovereign AI and ONDC integration, which includes developing their own designed-in-house servers.

history

Zoho Corporation is an Indian multinational technology company that makes cloud-based business software.[6] Its core products include workplace productivity and collaboration software, customer relationship management (CRM) software, and business management tools[7] .The company was founded in 1996 by Sridhar Vembu and Tony Thomas as AdventNet, Inc., in New Jersey.[8] Zoho is headquartered in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India, and has offices in 80 countries, with its US headquarters located in Austin, Texas [9] . As of 2023, it is privately owned,[10] with co-founders Tony Thomas and Sridhar Vembu holding 8% and 5% stakes, and Sridhar Vembu's sister Radha Vembu and brother Sekar Vembu owning 47.8% and 35.2% of the company, respectively [11][12] .

From 1996 to 2009, the company was known as AdventNet, Inc., founded in New Jersey, US, and initially provided a network management software called WebNMS [4][13].

AdventNet expanded operations into Japan in 2001 and shifted focus to small and medium businesses (SMBs).[13] In 2002, the company started its enterprise IT management software division known as ManageEngine [14] .

Zoho CRM was released in 2005, along with Zoho Writer, the company's first office suite product.[13] Zoho Projects, Creator, Sheet, and Show were released in 2006.[13] It expanded into the collaboration space with the release of Zoho Docs and Zoho Meeting in 2007. In 2008, the company added invoicing and mail applications.[15] In 2009, the company was renamed Zoho Corporation after its online office suite.[4] The company remains privately owned.[16]

In 2014, Zoho had its first annual user conference, called Zoholics, in India.[17][7] In 2017, Zoho launched Zoho One, a platform of over 40 integrated applications.[18] In 2020, it launched Zoho Workplace, a suite combining its existing productivity, collaboration, and communication software.[19]

In 2023, ERP software company Gofrugal, founded by Sridhar Vembu's brother Kumar Vembu, merged with Zoho.[20] In 2025, Zoho announced the launch of its large language model, Zia, for enterprise AI applications.[21]

Following a government tender won by Zoho in September 2023, all 1.2 million email accounts of Indian central government employees were migrated from the NIC-based system to Zoho Mail by 2025, while office productivity tools were shifted to the Zoho Workplace suite.[22][23]

In January 2025, co-founder Shailesh Kumar Davey succeeded Sridhar Vembu as group CEO, while Vembu transitioned to the role of chief scientist to focus on research and development.[24] In January 2026, Zoho launched Zoho ERP, an enterprise resource planning product developed at its Kumbakonam, Tamil Nadu office.[25] The same month, the company opened its first data centers in the United Arab Emirates, in Dubai and Abu Dhabi.[citation needed]

In 2026 Zoho Unveils Nathu La, a Designed-in-House Server, server motherboard and chassis platform in a Move Towards Technological Sovereignty and Inference Cost Reduction[26].

product

Zoho has over 45 integrated applications, mainly designed for small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs), but scalable for larger enterprises. These products span many categories, including customer relationship management, marketing, sales, customer support, finance, human resources, collaboration, productivity, operations, IT management, and analytics. Notable products include the online office suite offering Zoho Office Suite, customer relationship management software Zoho CRM, accounting software Zoho Books, HR management platform Zoho People, and project management software Zoho Projects [17][33].
ManageEngine has both on-premises and cloud-based software products in categories such as enterprise IT management, cybersecurity, and unified endpoint management. [34][35]

Messaging app

Arattai is an instant messaging application developed by Zoho and launched in January 2021.[36] It experienced a brief surge in new user sign-ups in 2025 when Zoho products were promoted by Indian government officials as part of their push for homegrown alternatives to foreign‐owned apps.[37][38][39]

Browser

Ulaa Browser is a web browser developed by Zoho, based on the Chromium open-source project and released in 2023. Ulaa routes its Domain Name System (DNS) queries through India's national root server system, rather than using default global DNS providers.[40]

email

Zoho Mail is an email service developed by Zoho Corporation in 2008 for individuals and organizations. The email hosting service supports custom domain addresses and is integrated with other Zoho products within the Zoho Workplace suite.[41][42]

Payment app

Zoho Pay, a UPI-based payments and money transfer application integrated with its messaging platform Arattai and available as a standalone mobile app in India. Zoho pay is currently in testing and expected to release soon.[43][44][45]

TCS

(TCS) is a global IT services, consulting, and business solutions organization headquartered in Mumbai, India.
As the flagship company of the Tata Group, it is India’s largest IT services company and one of the largest employers in the technology industry worldwide. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5] Core Offerings & Focus Areas

  • AI & Data Solutions: Leading AI-led transformations from infrastructure to intelligence.
  • Industry Verticals: Serves sectors like BFSI (Banking, Financial Services, and Insurance), retail, life sciences, and manufacturing.

history

1968–2000 TCS Delta Park in Salt Lake Sector-V, Greater Kolkata
Tata Consultancy Services Limited, originally known as Tata Computer Systems, was established in 1968 by Tata Sons Limited.[1] The company's initial contracts involved providing punched card services to its sister company TISCO (now Tata Steel), developing an Inter-Branch Reconciliation System for the Central Bank of India,[2] and offering bureau services to the Unit Trust of India.
In 1975, TCS implemented an electronic depository and trading system named SECOM for Swiss company SIS SegaInterSettle. It also developed System X for the Canadian Depository System and automated the Johannesburg Stock Exchange.[3] TCS also partnered with the Swiss firm TKS Teknosoft, which it later acquired.[4]
In 1980, TCS established India's first dedicated software research and development center, the Tata Research Development and Design Centre (TRDDC), located in Pune.[5] The following year, it created India's first client-dedicated offshore development centre, established for Tandem.
Anticipating the Y2K bug and the introduction of the unified European currency (Euro), Tata Consultancy Services developed a factory model for Y2K conversion. The company also created software tools to automate the conversion process and facilitate implementation by third-party developers and clients.[6] In late 1999, TCS introduced Decision Support System (DSS) solutions to the domestic market.[7] In 1999, the company also registered its first tagline, "Beyond the Obvious."[8][9]

2001–2019 In 2001, TCS entered the bioinformatics segment,[10] and three years later, launched India's first bioinformatics product.[11]
In 2003, TCS became the first Indian IT company to record $1 billion in revenue.[12] On 25 August 2004, TCS became a publicly listed company after its initial public offering.[13][14]
In July 2005, Tata Infotech, which was until then a different IT subsidiary of Tata Sons, merged with TCS in a stock swap deal.[15] Later that year, TCS changed its tagline from "Beyond the Obvious" to "Experience Certainty".[16]
In 2006, TCS developed an ERP system for the Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation.[17] By 2008, its e-business operations were generating over US$500 million in annual revenue.[18][19]
In 2011, TCS entered the small and medium enterprises market with cloud-based solutions.[20] On the final trading day of 2011, it surpassed RIL to achieve the highest market capitalization of any India-based company.[21] In the 2011–12 fiscal year, TCS achieved annual revenues exceeding US$10 billion for the first time.[22]
In May 2013, TCS was awarded a six-year contract valued at over ₹11 billion (US$120 million) to provide services to the Indian Department of Posts.[23] In 2013, the company moved from 13th to 10th place on the list of global IT services companies by revenue.[24] In July 2014, it became the first Indian company to exceed ₹5 trillion (US$81.93 billion) in market capitalization.[25][26]
In January 2015, TCS became India's most profitable company, ending Reliance Industries Limited's 23-year streak.[27]
In January 2017, TCS announced a partnership with Aurus, a payments technology company, to deliver payment solutions for retailers through TCS OmniStore, a unified store commerce platform.[28] The same year, TCS China entered into a joint venture with the Chinese government.[29]
In March 2018, Tata Sons sold TCS shares worth $1.25 billion in a bulk transaction.[30]
In 2019, TCS received four Stevies at the American Business Awards.[31]

2020–present On 8 October 2020, TCS surpassed Accenture in market capitalization, becoming the world's most valuable IT company with a market capitalization of over $144 billion.[32] On 25 January 2021, TCS briefly reclaimed the title of the world's most valuable IT company from Accenture with a market cap of $170 billion.[33] On the same day, TCS also became India's most valuable company, surpassing Reliance Industries.[34]
In May 2021, TCS, in collaboration with its consortium partner Neurotechnology, was selected by the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) to provide biometric technology for the Aadhaar digital ID program.[35] The Aadhaar program, which has a database of over 1.3 billion citizens, has been described by World Bank Chief Economist Paul Romer as the "most sophisticated ID program in the world."[36]
In 2021, TCS underwent a millennial rebranding, and the company updated its tagline from "Experience Certainty" to "Building on Belief".[37] In 2021, Tata Consultancy Services was also one of the largest job providers in India, hiring 43,000 new employees in the first half of the fiscal year 2021–22.[38]
In July 2025, TCS announced that it would downsize its global workforce by 2%, or about 12,000 employees, primarily from middle- and senior-level positions, citing a "skill mismatch".[39][40] By the end of the 2025–26 fiscal year, the company reported a consolidated revenue of ₹271,423 crore (US$28 billion).[41] The company reported a global headcount of 584,519 employees as of March 2026.[42]

infosys

Infosys Limited is an Indian multinational technology company that offers information technology, business consulting, and outsourcing services.Founded in 1981 by seven engineers, the company is headquartered in Bengaluru and considered one of the Big Six Indian IT companies. Infosys has also attracted controversies due to allegations of visa and tax fraud in the United States and for creating malfunctioning government websites.

service and product

Infosys offers software development, maintenance, and independent validation services across industries such as finance, insurance, manufacturing, among others.[35] Through its subsidiary Infosys Consulting, it provides consulting services in digital experience, cloud,[36] data analytics, artificial intelligence, engineering,[37] and sustainability.[38] Its subsidiary Infosys BPM provides outsourcing services for business processes such as finance, procurement, customer service, and HR.[39]
Infosys offers digital products and platforms for digital transformation, including digital banking software Finacle, application delivery platform Panaya, digital commerce platform Infosys Equinox, workplace platform Infosys Meridian, and customer engagement platform Infosys Cortex.[40][41] It also provides sets of services and platforms in domains such as cloud with Cobalt,[42] generative AI with Topaz,[43] and AI marketing with Aster.[44]
