ECE department

ECE stands for Electronics and Communication Engineering, an engineering discipline focusing on electronic devices, microchips, and communication systems like 5G, satellites, and IoT. It bridges hardware and software to build modern tech like smartphones and computers

EEE department

The EEE department stands for the Electrical and Electronics Engineering department. It focuses on power systems, electrical machines, and electronic devices

Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE) involves the generation, transmission, and distribution of power, alongside the design and testing of electronic circuits and control systems. EEE professionals build and manage large-scale energy grids, green energy systems, and automated machinery.

mechanical department

A mechanical department is an academic or industrial unit focused on the design, analysis, manufacturing, and maintenance of physical machines and thermal systems. It uses core principles of physics, mathematics, and material science to build everything from small tools to large engines.

Mechanical engineering is the study of physical machines and mechanisms that may involve force and movement. It is an engineering branch that combines engineering physics and mathematics principles with materials science, to design, analyze, manufacture, and maintain mechanical systems.[1] It is one of the oldest and broadest of the engineering branches.
